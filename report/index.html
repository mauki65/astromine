About this report

Orbits, sizes, spectral types, radar measurements and close approaches come from the NASA/JPL Small-Body Database; round-trip trajectories are computed hourly for every mining candidate (NASA NHATS and a Hohmann estimate fill in for the rest). Composition is inferred from asteroid type and meteorite analogues — only a handful of asteroids have been visited. Prices: gold-api.com (Au, Ag, Pt, Pd, Cu, live), Umicore (Rh, Ir, Ru), reference values for the rest. This is an exploration tool, not investment advice.
